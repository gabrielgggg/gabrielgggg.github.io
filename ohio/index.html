This dashboard reports forecasts from a hierarchical mixed-frequency dynamic factor model (MF-DFM) estimated on Ohio, Midwest, and national series from FRED. Growth series enter as log differences; interest rates and unemployment enter as period changes (Δr) and are cumulated back to levels for display. Charts show either rate levels or year-over-year percent growth (never quarter-over-quarter or month-over-month).

Factor structure

  • National factors: 2 (all series load on these)
  • Ohio residual factors: 2 (Ohio/regional series, after national)
  • Block factors: 1 per block (labor, prices, housing, activity, national; Ohio series only)
  • Total factors in state: 8 (national1, national2, ohio1, ohio2, labor1, prices1, housing1, activity1)

National series load only on national factors. Ohio series load on national + Ohio + their block factors. Factors are extracted stagewise (national first, then residualize, then Ohio and blocks).

Factor dynamics

  • Process: shared VAR/AR order AR(6) (selected by sum of BIC over candidates [1, 2, 3, 4, 6])
  • Forecast horizon: 12 months
  • Confidence bands: Monte Carlo (350 sims); 68% (16th–84th percentiles) and faint outer 95% (2.5th–97.5th)
  • History window on charts: about 3 years, always starting in January

Sample & robustness

  • Estimation sample: 1950-01-31 to 2026-06-30 (137 series)
  • Factor sample start: 2000-01-01
  • COVID handling: dummies (2020-03-01–2020-12-31; soft tail to 2021-06-30)
  • Growth winsorization: 0.5% tails
