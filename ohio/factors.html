Hierarchical DFM factor paths

Data through Aug 2026 · Updated Aug 7, 2026

11 factors · history from 2000 · COVID window blanked (line breaks) · forecast 12 months

These are latent statistical factors from the hierarchical mixed-frequency DFM used on the Ohio economy page—not plotted series. Soft titles are interpretation aids from the largest absolute loadings in the current estimate.

  • Sign is arbitrary (weakly normalized); higher is not automatically “better.”
  • Scale is standardized (near mean zero in sample), not percent growth or rate points.
  • MSA and block factors are residuals after higher layers (national, then Ohio, then local/sector).
  • Labels can shift when loadings re-estimate; technical ids (national1, …) stay stable.
  • Plots start in 2000. The hard/soft COVID window is missing on the chart (line breaks, not connected through the gap) so 2020–21 spikes do not dominate the scale.
